# Product Management & Consulting Competencies Demonstrated

**Project**: Zomato Tiffin Service Aggregation & Cloud Kitchen Ecosystem  
**Strategist**: **Ayush Shah**  

---

### 1. Market Sizing & Quantitative Modeling
- Engineered multi-layered top-down and bottom-up market sizing models dissecting India's 1.4B population into urban young adults (12%) and students (10%), identifying a **$3.2B addressable market** and **Rs. 613B domestic opportunity**.
- Calculated delivery fleet guesstimates across 3 national expansion phases (25,000 to 40,000 delivery partners based on peak shift order densities).

### 2. User Research & Empirical Synthesis
- Executed primary quantitative surveys (Google Forms) and offline 1-on-1 interviews with corporate professionals, hostel students, and tiffin cooks.
- Translated friction points into a weighted 4-factor prioritization matrix ($Impact + Frequency + Urgency + Business Value$).

### 3. Business Architecture & Unit Economics
- Modeled complete commercial viability for a 350 sq. ft. turnkey cloud kitchen franchise, factoring rent, utilities, raw materials (Rs. 35/box), labor, and amortized licensing to yield a **17.6% net margin (Rs. 5,300/day net profit)**.
- Formulated platform commission schedules across Tier-1 and Tier-2 clusters generating a **+$450M annual revenue projection for Zomato**.

### 4. Technical System Design & AI Solutions
- Designed the end-to-end technical architecture for **VendorVision AI BizChat**, utilizing LLM intent extraction and Natural Language-to-SQL querying against MySQL seller databases to deliver real-time operational insights to non-tech-savvy cooks.
- Architected the automated cancellation, flash-reselling, and Zomato Feeding India NGO redistribution state machine.

### 5. Product Roadmapping & Growth Strategy
- Structured a 12-month, 4-phase rollout plan aligning engineering, logistics, real estate leasing, and geographic expansion.
- Formulated growth campaigns including Homemaker FOMO workshops, Milestone Badges (Rising Star, Customer Favorite, Elite), and corporate cafeteria QR bottle sponsorships.
