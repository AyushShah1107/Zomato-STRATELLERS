# Cloud Kitchen & Tiffin Unit Economics Model

**Project**: Zomato Tiffin Aggregation & Cloud Kitchen Franchise  
**Author**: Ayush Shah (Lead Product Strategist)  

---

## 1. 350 sq. ft. Turnkey Cloud Kitchen Model (Daily P&L)

### Baseline Assumptions
- **Kitchen Footprint**: 350 sq. ft. commercial space in high-density office / student corridor.
- **Daily Order Volume**: 500 completed meal boxes per day.
- **Average Order Value (AOV)**: Rs. 60 per meal.
- **Gross Daily Sales**: 500 orders × Rs. 60 = **Rs. 30,000 / day**.

### Daily Operating Expenses (OPEX)
| Expense Category | Daily Cost (INR) | Monthly Equiv. (INR) | Operational Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Kitchen Lease / Rent** | Rs. 1,600 | Rs. 48,000 | 350 sq. ft. semi-commercial hub location |
| **Electricity, Gas & Utilities** | Rs. 1,000 | Rs. 30,000 | Commercial induction, refrigerators, exhaust |
| **Ingredients & Packaging** | Rs. 17,500 | Rs. 5,25,000 | Rs. 35 per box (Rs. 28 fresh produce + Rs. 7 eco box) |
| **Kitchen Unit Manager** | Rs. 1,600 | Rs. 48,000 | Supervises inventory, FSSAI compliance, dispatch |
| **Workforce Staff (3 Cooks)** | Rs. 3,000 | Rs. 90,000 | 3 dedicated trained culinary staff (Rs. 30k/mo each) |
| **FSSAI State License & Misc** | Rs. 14 | Rs. 416 | Rs. 5,000 annual licensing amortized daily |
| **Total Daily Operating Cost** | **Rs. 24,714** | **Rs. 7,41,416** | **Cost per delivered meal = Rs. 49.40** |

---

## 2. Daily & Annual Profitability

```
Daily Revenue:                Rs. 30,000
Daily Operating Cost:       - Rs. 24,714
────────────────────────────────────────
Daily Net Operating Profit:   Rs.  5,286 / day (~Rs. 5,300)

Monthly Net Profit (30 Days): Rs. 1,58,580 (~Rs. 1.6 Lakhs)
Annual Net Profit per Unit:   Rs. 19,02,960 (~$23,000 USD)
Net Operating Margin:         17.6%
```

## 3. Platform Aggregation Economics (Zomato Marketplace Model)

For home-based cooks and external vendors utilizing the Zomato marketplace:
- **Take Rate / Commission**: 15% of order value.
- **Average Monthly User Spend**: Rs. 3,000 (Rs. 50/meal × 2 meals/day × 30 days).
- **Zomato Commission per User**: Rs. 450 / month.

### Scale Projections
- **Tier-1 Cities (0.5M active subscribers)**:
  - Monthly Platform Gross Revenue = Rs. 225 Crores.
  - Annualized Gross Revenue = Rs. 2,700 Crores.
- **Tier-2 Cities (0.25M active subscribers)**:
  - Monthly Platform Gross Revenue = Rs. 113 Crores.
  - Annualized Gross Revenue = Rs. 1,350 Crores.
- **Combined Annual Platform Run-Rate**: **Rs. 4,050 Crores (~$485 Million USD)**.
